A preregistered empirical and computational study investigating metacognitive uncertainty in correct and error trials across the post-decisional response window using a Random Dot Motion (RDM) task. We examine whether the duration of the post-decisional window remains associated with metacognitive uncertainty after explicitly accounting for action-related processes within a Signal Detection Theory framework.
